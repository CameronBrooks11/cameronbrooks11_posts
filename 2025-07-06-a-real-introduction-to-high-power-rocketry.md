# A Real Introduction to High-Power Rocketry

While preparing for my L2 and looking for resources to support new team members on the rocketry team at Western University I was frustrated by the seeming lack of straight forward, general purpose material covering the fundamentals of HPR. Something that can be read and understood in an hour or two tops with enough theoretical background to satisfy an engineering student while still remaining accessible to the average person looking to take up the hobby to get that thrill they miss from their day job. This is what I will aim to do here with a particular focus on the knowledge required to pass the L2 written test for both technical and safety questions.
